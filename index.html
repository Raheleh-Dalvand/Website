About Me

Hi! I'm Raheleh, passionate about all things data — from data engineering to data analytics. My experience spans data engineering, data analytics, business intelligence, and cloud data platforms, with a focus on designing end-to-end data solutions using Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, Azure Synapse Analytics, SQL, Python, and Power BI.

I hold a Master’s Degree in Information Technology and a Bachelor’s Degree in Software Engineering, complemented by Microsoft certifications in Azure Data Engineering, Power BI, and Fabric Analytics Engineering.

This website is my personal learning journey, where I explore, experiment, and share what I'm learning and working on in the world of data. Whether it's analyzing trends, building models, or designing data pipelines, I'm always excited to dive deeper and grow in this ever-evolving field. Feel free to explore and connect!
